Notes:
One of the greatest Konami soundtracks ever to
grace the Game Boy DMG, it features a number of
original tracks with the usual Konami style,
along with a number of arrangements of Danny
Elfman's Batman theme from the 1989 movie.

Kurahashi is credited for "music image" while
Hagiwara is solely credited for music
composition. I don't know if "music image"
means anything, but Kurahashi *is* credited in
the GBS, so she's listed here as well.

No Japanese tags here-- this game was not
released in Japan.
